# Security policy

This is the default security policy for every **systemonedev** repository that doesn't have its own.
[SystemOne Builder](https://github.com/systemonedev/systemone-builder/blob/main/SECURITY.md) has a more
detailed one.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue** for a vulnerability. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting
(the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**), or email **systemonedev@gmail.com**
with "SECURITY" in the subject. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version, commit
or URL.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 working days and to agree on a fix and disclosure timeline
with you. We credit reporters in release notes unless you'd rather we didn't.

## Scope

The code in our repositories, the published `systemone-client` package, the models published at
[huggingface.co/systemonedev](https://huggingface.co/systemonedev) and the systemone.dev website.

Wrong or poorly calibrated model answers are quality bugs, not vulnerabilities: please open a normal
issue with an example.
